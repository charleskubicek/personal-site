### Director of Data Enablement — Springer Nature
*January 2023 – Present*

I lead data engineering and data enablement for a function serving 40 domain teams across Springer Nature — translating executive ambition around AI and data into concrete foundations that teams can actually build on. I own the data engineering competence directly (8 engineers) and operate in a federated model with 15 more embedded across the business.

- Moved the organisation from duplicated, non-standard data infrastructure to a shared platform: 50 teams now hosted on BigQuery with standardised tooling, delivery practices, and data standards for interoperability.
- Reduced shadow IT and waste by establishing a federated ownership model — domain teams own their data products; the central function sets standards and enables delivery rather than owning everything poorly.
- Scaled data platform adoption to 40 teams while keeping costs reasonable through clear federated cost accountability.
- Shipped reusable data products, including one now consumed by 30 teams — a concrete example of the shift from bespoke delivery to reusable, governed assets that can be used as geniune foundations for wide-ranging use cases, beyond the analytical plane.
